\section{Per-benchmark results and sensitivity}
\label{sec:perbench}

\subsection{Per benchmark}

Table~\ref{tab:perbench} gives each benchmark's sample and estimate.
Five point estimates lie above 1 and five below, and they range from \BenchJOR{} on \bench{gpsanant/ecdsafail-challenge} to \BenchCOR{} on \bench{eigenlabs/eip8200-challenges/modexp}.
Informative solvers per benchmark range from 19 to 46. On nine benchmarks some of them have primary rows that are all cross-family or all same-family, and those solvers inform the covariates only.

The promotion rule was reproduced on \ATFiveReproduced{} benchmarks (Section~\ref{sec:outcomes}).
On \ATFiveNotReproducedList{}, recomputation disagrees with the recorded status on more than 1\% of rows, and Y1 comes from the recorded status as the pre-registration requires.

\subsection{Sensitivity analyses}

Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} and Figure~\ref{fig:sens} recompute the pooled estimate under each of the nine pre-registered changes.
Every pooled odds ratio lies between 1.01 and 1.03, and every 90\% interval lies inside the margin, so no single change moves the verdict.
Analysis 2 estimates a different quantity, the effect of the immediate code parent rather than the declared origin, and agrees with the primary.
Analysis 7 is identical to the primary: the author's \AuthorRows{} submissions are all on ECDSA.Fail, and the three that are analysis rows predate its confirmatory window.
Analysis 8 keeps one member of each sibling pair and pools eight benchmarks.

Unknown labels concentrate on one benchmark.
On \bench{eigenlabs/flock-challenge}, \FlockUnknownCell{} of \FlockAnalysisRows{} analysis rows are cross-solver rows with an Unknown or Multi family on one side, and in \FlockUnknownParent{} of them it is the parent's family that is missing.
Analysis 1, which treats Unknown as a family of its own, moves that benchmark's odds ratio from \BenchEOR{} [\BenchEORLow{}, \BenchEORHigh{}] to \FlockSOneOR{} [\FlockSOneLow{}, \FlockSOneHigh{}].
This per-benchmark figure is descriptive; the pre-registered analysis 1 reports only the pooled value.
